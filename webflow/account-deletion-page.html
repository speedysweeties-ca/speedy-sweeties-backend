Delete your Speedy Sweeties customer account

You can ask us to delete your customer account and eligible information without placing another order.

If you still have the app

On iPhone or iPad, open Account, then choose Delete Account. We will send a fresh verification code to an eligible contact already connected to your customer record. Follow the app to confirm the request and keep its private status credential so you can check progress. If your app version does not offer this control, use the email request path below.

If you no longer have the app or original contact

Email speedysweeties@hotmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request.” Tell us your own name and the contact method where Robert may respond. You may include an order or receipt number to help locate your request, but do not email passwords, payment-card details, government identification, or another person's information.

Robert handles exceptional recovery privately. Order details can help find a record but do not prove that every historical order belongs to you. Shared, changed, reassigned, or conflicting phone numbers and email addresses are placed on hold unless ownership can be established. We will not disclose another customer’s information.

What happens next

  1. We record the request and verify it against an existing customer and source order using an existing verified channel where available.
  2. After verification, we normally complete eligible deletion within 30 calendar days. An ownership conflict, active delivery, dispute, or applicable legal hold may require longer and will be handled separately.
  3. We remove eligible profile, preference and loyalty information and revoke account access. We also prevent an old backup or app copy from silently reactivating the deleted account.
  4. We confirm completion and explain what information was retained.

Information that may be retained

Restricted receipt and supporting transaction evidence is generally kept for six years after the end of the last tax year it relates to, or longer when an applicable hold requires it. Our fiscal year-end is December 31; the calculation does not start from your deletion-request date. This may include record, order and receipt numbers; dates; payment method and final status; item descriptions, quantities and prices; receipt totals, charges and tax; and recognized receipt-charge facts. It is restricted to justified financial, legal, accounting, or dispute purposes and is not used for marketing or to recreate a deleted profile.

Minimized request and audit evidence is first reviewed 24 calendar months after the request closes. The anti-reactivation marker that protects against restored data recreating a deleted account is first reviewed 24 calendar months after database erasure. These are review dates, not minimum retention periods or automatic deletion dates. Information is deleted when no justified need remains; any extension has a documented reason and next review date. Anti-reactivation protection is preserved while a verified restoration or unresolved-case risk remains.

We review expiry and any hold before removing or irreversibly anonymizing retained records. We do not automatically delete an account merely because it has been inactive for two years.
